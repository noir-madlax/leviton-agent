**CONSOLIDATION RULES:**
1. **Full deduplication goal** - The primary purpose is to eliminate ALL duplicates and near-duplicates across both taxonomies, creating a single unified taxonomy with no redundancy. 2. **Group similar categories** - Combine categories that represent the same or very similar product types based on their definitions, regardless of which taxonomy they came from
3. **Preserve specificity and handle unique categories** - Maintain the specific nature of each category type rather than creating broader, more generic categories. If a category is truly unique and has no similar counterparts in either taxonomy, it can remain as a single-item group

6. **Complete mapping** - Every A_* and B_* ID must appear exactly once in the "ids" arrays - no duplicates or missing IDs
7. **Maintain consistent granularity** - ALL consolidated categories must be at the same level of specificity and detail. Ensure no category is significantly more general or more specific than others - aim for uniform granularity across the entire final taxonomy
8. **Ensure mutual exclusivity** - Categories must have clear, non-overlapping boundaries with no ambiguity
9. **Write focused definitions** - Combine the original definitions from all merged categories, then refine/clean up redundancies to produce one precise definition that clearly describes the specific product type, ending with "e.g." and specific product subtypes (NEVER include brand names, model numbers, or proprietary product line names)
